Learning Paths

The platform hosts 86 interactive demos across 24 pages. That's a lot to navigate cold. These curated paths sequence the best demos for each role — a senior consultant's recommended reading order. Pick a path, follow it step by step, and your progress persists in this browser.

Quant trader onboarding
From market data to risk — the first 12 demos a new quant should see
Advanced

A curated path through the financial-services demos, ordered the way a senior quant would walk a new hire through the desk: start with how market data arrives (ticks → OHLCV), then how options are priced (Black-Scholes), how the volatility surface is constructed, how risk is measured (VaR/ES), how strategies are built and backtested, and finally how execution quality is measured.

12 demos ~25 min

Data engineer foundations
From star schema to SCD2 to bridge tables — 15 demos that make a Kimball expert
Mixed

The dimensional modelling curriculum. Starts with the star schema (the foundational pattern), walks through SCD types (how history is tracked), fact table patterns (transaction, snapshot, accumulating, factless), then the challenging datasets that separate senior engineers from juniors: grain detective, bridge tables, role-playing dimensions, ragged hierarchies. Ends with governance (GDPR erasure) and the deep conceptual equivalence of event sourcing and SCD2.

15 demos ~30 min
Progress0%
Audience: Data engineers, analytics engineers, BI developers, warehouse architects
Life-sciences researcher
From VCF to quantum — 10 demos across biology, chemistry, neuroimaging, physics, quantum
Advanced

A cross-domain tour for researchers who work at the intersection of life sciences and physical sciences. Starts with genomics file standards (VCF, BGEN, PLINK), moves through molecular biology (alternative splicing, taxonomic graphs, viral mutation), into chemistry (stereoisomerism, phase boundaries), neuroimaging (fMRI BOLD + GLM), and ends with the physical-mathematical foundations (Schwarzschild metric, arbitrary precision, qubit superposition, quantum entanglement). Each demo includes the dimensional model for warehousing the data.

10 demos ~25 min
Progress0%
Audience: Bioinformaticians, computational biologists, research engineers, cross-domain scientists

DeFi quant explorer
From AMM curves to MEV — 6 demos covering DeFi fundamentals
Advanced

For quants and engineers moving from traditional finance into DeFi. GDPR & Data Protection Notice

This demonstration application is designed to operate without collecting personal data from visitors. No cookies are set for analytics or tracking purposes, and no personal information is transmitted to third parties. Theme preferences are stored locally in your browser only.

In line with the UK GDPR and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), any production deployment of the underlying dimensional model must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including data minimisation, purpose limitation, storage limitation, access controls, and lawful basis documentation for any personal data processed within the warehouse. Data subject rights — including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection — must be supported by the surrounding governance framework and reflected in the conformed dimension designs.

For data protection enquiries relating to this artefact, contact the repository owner via the email above. This notice is provided for demonstration purposes and should be reviewed by your Data Protection Officer before any production use.
