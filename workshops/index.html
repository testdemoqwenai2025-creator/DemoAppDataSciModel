Stakeholder Engagement

Data modelling workshops & stakeholder engagement

Dimensional models that are not co-designed with the people who will use them do not get adopted. This solution runs a structured series of workshops that turn business process understanding into engineering-ready model specifications — with stakeholder ownership captured at every step.

Why workshops, not documents

A modelling document handed over a wall tends to be read once and then ignored. A workshop where stakeholders draw the model with the modeller creates ownership: the business sees its terminology reflected in the dimension attributes, the engineers see the grain decisions explained, and the stewards see the quality rules they will own.

Every model in this solution is the output of at least one workshop. Workshops are short, focused and decision-oriented — their value is in the conversations they force, not the slides they produce.

The workshop series

Four workshop types cover the modelling lifecycle from discovery to sign-off. They are run in sequence per business process; the bus matrix workshop is run once for the whole programme.

Business process discovery
Workshop 1 · Half day · Business owners, process SMEs, modeller
Half day
Outcome

A prioritised list of business processes to model, each with a candidate grain and a set of business questions it must answer.

Agenda
  • Context-setting: what is a dimensional model, why we start from the business process
  • Process inventory: list every operational activity the organisation cares about
  • Prioritisation: score by business value, data readiness and feasibility
  • Question harvest: for the top processes, capture the questions users want to answer
Bus matrix workshop
Workshop 2 · Full day · Domain leads, enterprise architect, modeller, governance lead
Full day
Outcome

A populated enterprise bus matrix: business processes on rows, conformed dimensions on columns, ownership and priority assigned.

Agenda
  • Review the prioritised business processes
  • Identify the dimensions each process needs — who, what, where, when, why
  • Spot conformance opportunities: dimensions that appear in multiple processes
  • Assign owners and stewards per dimension; agree delivery priority
Dimension design studio
Workshop 3 · Half day per dimension · Dimension owner, steward, modeller, source SME
Half day per dimension
Outcome

A draft dimension design: grain, attributes, hierarchies, SCD types, source mapping and quality rules.

Agenda
  • Confirm the dimension grain and primary key
  • Harvest attributes from the source system and the business
  • Define hierarchies and rollup paths
  • Assign SCD type per attribute — Type 1, 2, 3 or 6
  • Capture data quality rules and glossary terms
Model review & sign-off
Workshop 4 · 90 minutes · Business owner, modeller, ETL lead, BI lead, governance lead
90 minutes
Outcome

A signed-off model specification ready for ETL engineering. Open issues logged with owners and due dates.

Agenda
  • Walk through the fact table grain and measures
  • Walk through each dimension, its attributes and SCD choices
  • Review the source-to-target mapping
  • Confirm data quality rules and glossary alignment
  • Capture open issues and assign owners

Engagement principles

The five principles below shape how every workshop in this solution is run.

1

Conversational over documentary — models are drawn live in the room, not handed over as PDFs. Stakeholders see their input land in the design immediately.

2

One decision at a time — each workshop has a single deliverable. Avoid combining process discovery with dimension design; the cognitive switch wastes time.

3

Right people in the room — a model signed off without the business owner is not signed off. Block the calendar before the workshop, not after.

4

Decisions recorded in the model — every workshop decision is captured as a comment, attribute or glossary term in the modelling tool, not in meeting minutes that drift.

5

Follow-through visible — workshop actions appear in the next sprint’s ETL backlog, with the workshop request as the traceability link.
