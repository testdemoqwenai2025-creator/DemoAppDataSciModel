Drones (UAV): GPS denial, wind shear, deconfliction, BVLOS

Drone fleets produce 1000 Hz telemetry, navigate via GPS that can be denied, fly through wind that can exceed climb rate, and must deconflict with each other in 3D airspace. BVLOS operations add 2-second satellite latency. This page covers the edge cases and corner cases, and the dimensional model that handles them.

The core insight

A drone pose is not a single number — it is a probability distribution. Every pose has a source (GPS, VIO, fused) and a covariance matrix. The dimensional answer: promote the source to a dimension, store the covariance as JSON, and let queries filter by source and confidence. When GPS is denied, the drone does not stop — it switches source, and the warehouse records the transition as an event.

For deconfliction, don't store every check — store only conflicts. A fleet of 200 drones does 19,900 checks per 100 ms; 99.99% are non-conflicts. The fact_deconfliction_check table stores only the 0.01% that found a conflict, plus the resolution action. Historical proximity analysis uses a streaming spatial join, not a warehouse query.

Edge cases

GPS Denial & Visual Odometry Fallback
Problem

A delivery drone enters an urban canyon where GPS multipath causes position jumps of 50+ metres. The navigation microservice switches to visual odometry (VIO), but VIO drifts 1% of distance travelled — after 1 km, the drone is 10 m off. The fact_pose_estimate table stores both GPS and VIO poses without indicating which is authoritative, causing downstream collision deconfliction to use stale GPS.

Impact

Drone flies into a building because the deconfliction service uses a 10-second-old GPS pose that was actually a multipath reflection. The safety investigation cannot determine which pose was used for the collision decision.

Dimensional solution

Add a dim_position_source dimension (GPS, GPS-RTK, VIO, VIO-GPS-fused, beacon). Every fact_pose_estimate row carries a source_key and a covariance matrix (stored as JSON). The deconfliction service queries only fused poses with covariance below a threshold. When GPS is denied, a fact_navigation_mode_change event is emitted with the old and new source — the warehouse tracks mode transitions, not just poses.

Wind Shear Mid-Flight Re-Planning
Problem

A drone encounters wind shear (sudden 15 m/s downdraft) that exceeds its climb rate. The flight-control microservice detects the wind via IMU + airspeed discrepancy and must re-plan the route within 2 seconds. But the route-planning microservice holds the original mission plan in memory and does not subscribe to wind events. The drone stalls.

Impact

The drone enters a stall, loses 30 m of altitude in 3 seconds, and triggers an automatic landing in a non-designated zone. The mission fails; the warehouse records a mission.aborted event but cannot correlate it with the wind shear because wind data is in a separate time-series store.

Dimensional solution

Add a dim_weather_condition dimension (calm, gusty, shear, storm) and a fact_wind_reading table (1 Hz from the drone's air data system). The route-planning service subscribes to wind.reading events and holds the current plan in a CQRS read model. When wind exceeds a threshold, the service emits a route.replanned event with the old and new plans. The warehouse joins fact_mission_event to fact_wind_reading via timestamp, producing a "wind shear caused this abort" query.

fact_wind_reading + fact_route_change

Multi-Drone Airspace Deconfliction
Problem

A swarm of 50 delivery drones operates in the same airspace. Each drone broadcasts its position every 100 ms. The deconfliction microservice must check all 50 drones against each other — 50 × 49 / 2 = 1,225 pairwise checks every 100 ms. As the fleet grows to 200, this becomes 19,900 checks per 100 ms — too slow for a single service.

Impact

At 200 drones, the deconfliction service falls behind. Two drones on intersecting paths are not warned in time. A near-miss occurs. The warehouse stores position data but cannot answer "which drones were within 10 m of each other at time T" without a massive spatial join.

Dimensional solution

Use a spatial index (R-tree or geohash bucket) in the deconfliction service — only check drones in the same geohash bucket. Add a dim_airspace_sector dimension (geohash-based). The warehouse stores fact_deconfliction_check (one row per pairwise check that found a conflict) — not every check, only conflicts. For historical analysis, a fact_proximity_event table stores "drone A and drone B were within X metres at time T", computed by a streaming spatial join (Flink) on the position topic.

fact_deconfliction_check + fact_proximity_event

Corner case: BVLOS Communication Latency Cascade

A BVLOS (beyond visual line of sight) delivery drone loses its 4G/5G connection mid-flight over a rural area, falling back to satellite (2-second latency). Simultaneously, wind shear hits, and another drone enters its deconfliction zone. The ground station is now 2 seconds behind reality.

Navigation

GPS is available but the ground station cannot receive the drone's position for 2 seconds; the drone must navigate autonomously using onboard VIO

Deconfliction

A second drone is approaching; the deconfliction service runs onboard (edge), but its plan is based on a 2-second-old position of the other drone received via satellite

Wind

Wind shear exceeds climb rate; the flight controller must re-plan, but the new plan cannot be validated by the ground station for 2 seconds

Failure mode

The drone receives a deconfliction command based on a 2-second-old position of the other drone. The other drone has since moved. The command sends the first drone into the path of the second. The ground station sees the collision 2 seconds after it happens. The warehouse cannot reconstruct the timeline because the onboard event log was lost in the crash.

Solution

Every drone carries an edge event logger (persistent storage, crash-survivable). The logger records every decision with a monotonic clock and a GPS timestamp. When the drone lands (or crashes), the log is synced to the warehouse. The fact_bvlos_event table stores every communication-latency episode with its duration, cause, and recovery. The saga coordinator on the ground assigns a bvlos_episode_key; the onboard logger tags events with that key. Reproducibility is a join on bvlos_episode_key — not an argument about what the ground station saw.

Dimensional model (SQL DDL)
Position source, weather condition, airspace sector (geohash), BVLOS episode dimensions.
-- Drone robotics dimensional model
-- Handles GPS denial, wind shear, multi-drone deconfliction, BVLOS latency

-- Position source dimension (GPS, VIO, fused, beacon)
CREATE TABLE dim_position_source (
  source_key      INT PRIMARY KEY,
  source_name      VARCHAR(30),   -- GPS, GPS-RTK, VIO, VIO-GPS-fused, beacon
  accuracy_m       DECIMAL(8,3),  -- typical accuracy in metres
  drift_rate       DECIMAL(8,5),  -- drift per km travelled (VIO = 0.01)
  is_autonomous    BOOLEAN        -- true if source works without ground comms
);

-- Weather condition dimension
CREATE TABLE dim_weather_condition (
  weather_key      INT PRIMARY KEY,
  condition        VARCHAR(20),   -- calm, gusty, shear, storm
  wind_speed_range VARCHAR(20),   -- 0-5, 5-10, 10-15, 15+
  visibility_m     INT,
  is_safe_for_vtol BOOLEAN
);

-- Airspace sector dimension (geohash-based)
CREATE TABLE dim_airspace_sector (
  sector_key      INT PRIMARY KEY,
  geohash         VARCHAR(12),    -- e.g. "gcpvj"
  center_lat      DECIMAL(10,6),
  center_lon      DECIMAL(10,6),
  is_restricted   BOOLEAN,
  max_altitude_m  INT             -- ceiling for this sector
);

-- BVLOS episode dimension
CREATE TABLE dim_bvlos_episode (
  bvlos_key       INT PRIMARY KEY,
  drone_key       INT,
  start_time      TIMESTAMP,
  end_time        TIMESTAMP,
  latency_ms      INT,            -- round-trip latency
  fallback_link   VARCHAR(20),    -- satellite, mesh, none
  cause           VARCHAR(30)     -- 4G-loss, 5G-loss, satellite-degraded
);

-- Fact: pose estimate (with position source + covariance)
CREATE TABLE fact_pose_estimate (
  pose_key        BIGINT PRIMARY KEY,
  drone_key       INT,
  mission_key     INT,
  source_key      INT REFERENCES dim_position_source,
  bvlos_key       INT REFERENCES dim_bvlos_episode,  -- NULL if not in BVLOS
  timestamp       TIMESTAMP,
  latitude        DECIMAL(12,8),
  longitude       DECIMAL(12,8),
  altitude_m      DECIMAL(8,2),
  covariance_json TEXT,           -- 6x6 position covariance matrix
  confidence      DECIMAL(5,4)    -- 0.0 to 1.0
);

-- Fact: wind reading (from air data system)
CREATE TABLE fact_wind_reading (
  wind_key        BIGINT PRIMARY KEY,
  drone_key       INT,
  timestamp       TIMESTAMP,
  weather_key     INT REFERENCES dim_weather_condition,
  wind_speed_ms   DECIMAL(6,2),
  wind_dir_deg    DECIMAL(6,2),
  vertical_ms     DECIMAL(6,2),   -- downdraft (negative) or updraft
  is_shear        BOOLEAN          -- true if delta exceeds threshold
);

-- Fact: deconfliction check (only conflicts stored, not all checks)
CREATE TABLE fact_deconfliction_check (
  check_key       BIGINT PRIMARY KEY,
  drone_a_key     INT,
  drone_b_key     INT,
  sector_key      INT REFERENCES dim_airspace_sector,
  timestamp       TIMESTAMP,
  separation_m    DECIMAL(8,2),
  severity_key    INT,            -- safe, caution, warning, critical
  action_key      INT,            -- none, altitude-change, speed-change, hover
  time_to_cpa_s   DECIMAL(6,2)    -- time to closest point of approach
);
Drone fleet analytics notebook
Run this in the JupyterLite REPL to simulate a 20-drone delivery fleet and compute battery, wind, GPS source and collision-risk analytics.
# Drone Fleet Analytics — JupyterLite Notebook
# Run this in the JupyterLite REPL on /notebooks

import numpy as np
import pandas as pd

# --- Simulate a drone delivery fleet ---
np.random.seed(42)

drones = [f"DRONE-{i:03d}" for i in range(20)]
missions = ["delivery", "inspection", "survey", "return"]

# Generate 1 hour of 1 Hz fleet state
n_rows = 3600 * 20  # 1 hour × 20 drones
data = {
    "timestamp": pd.date_range("2026-01-01", periods=3600, freq="1s").repeat(20),
    "drone_id": np.tile(drones, 3600),
    "mission": np.random.choice(missions, n_rows, p=[0.4, 0.2, 0.2, 0.2]),
    "latitude": 51.5074 + np.random.randn(n_rows) * 0.01,
    "longitude": -0.1278 + np.random.randn(n_rows) * 0.01,
    "altitude_m": np.maximum(0, 80 + np.random.randn(n_rows) * 15),
    "ground_speed_ms": np.maximum(0, 12 + np.random.randn(n_rows) * 3),
    "battery_pct": np.maximum(0, 100 - np.random.randint(0, 95, n_rows)),
    "wind_speed_ms": np.maximum(0, 5 + np.random.randn(n_rows) * 3),
    "gps_source": np.random.choice(["GPS", "VIO", "fused"], n_rows, p=[0.7, 0.1, 0.2]),
    "comm_link": np.random.choice(["4G", "5G", "satellite"], n_rows, p=[0.3, 0.6, 0.1]),
}

df = pd.DataFrame(data)

# --- Fleet summary ---
print("=== Fleet Summary ===")
print(f"Total drones: {df['drone_id'].nunique()}")
print(f"Mission duration: 1 hour")
print(f"Total telemetry rows: {len(df):,}")
print()

# --- Battery analysis ---
print("=== Battery Analysis ===")
battery = df.groupby("drone_id")["battery_pct"].agg(["min", "mean", "max"])
low_battery = battery[battery["min"] < 20]
print(f"Drones with <20% battery: {len(low_battery)}")
print(low_battery.head())
print()

# --- GPS source distribution ---
print("=== Navigation Source ===")
print(df["gps_source"].value_counts(normalize=True).round(3))
print()

# --- Communication link distribution ---
print("=== Communication Link ===")
print(df["comm_link"].value_counts(normalize=True).round(3))
print()

# --- Wind analysis (potential shear events) ---
print("=== Wind Analysis ===")
wind = df.groupby("timestamp")["wind_speed_ms"].mean()
shear_events = wind[wind > 12]  # >12 m/s = potential shear
print(f"Potential wind shear events (>12 m/s): {len(shear_events)}")
if len(shear_events) > 0:
    print(f"  Peak wind: {shear_events.max():.1f} m/s at {shear_events.idxmax()}")
print()

# --- Altitude distribution (collision risk) ---
print("=== Altitude Distribution ===")
alt = df["altitude_m"]
print(f"  Mean: {alt.mean():.1f} m")
print(f"  Std:  {alt.std():.1f} m")
print(f"  Min:  {alt.min():.1f} m")
print(f"  Max:  {alt.max():.1f} m")
print(f"  Drones below 50m (landing/takeoff risk): {(df['altitude_m'] < 50).sum():,} readings")

Microservices decomposition
  • drone-state-service — owns dim_robot, fact_pose_estimate. Subscribes to GPS + VIO; emits pose events with source_key.
  • weather-service — owns dim_weather_condition, fact_wind_reading. Subscribes to air data; emits wind.shear events.
  • deconfliction-service — owns dim_airspace_sector, fact_deconfliction_check. Spatial index; emits conflict events only.
  • bvlos-service — owns dim_bvlos_episode. Tracks link quality; emits episode events on degradation.
  • saga-coordinator — assigns bvlos_episode_key; ensures onboard logger syncs on landing.
