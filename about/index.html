About

About this dimensional data warehouse solution

A reference implementation and engagement model for delivering enterprise-scale Kimball dimensional data warehouses. Built to be previewed publicly without an NDA, with advanced modifications maintained in a private repository.

Mission

This solution exists to make enterprise dimensional data warehousing accessible, consistent and future-ready. We design and maintain dimensional models using the Kimball methodology — developing and enhancing fact tables, conformed dimensions, hierarchies, bus matrices and star schemas — and we wrap those models in the governance, documentation and stakeholder engagement needed for them to succeed in a complex organisation.

The application works closely with architects, engineers, analysts and business stakeholders to deliver high-quality data solutions. It is built to support enterprise reporting, self-service analytics and the data platform patterns required for future AI/ML enablement — not just the dashboards of today, but the models and features of tomorrow.

Design principles

The four principles below shape every model, every workshop and every specification we produce.

Business-process first

We start from the operational processes the organisation cares about — sales, claims, shipments, registrations — and let the grain of those processes drive every downstream modelling decision. This keeps the warehouse anchored to real business questions rather than to source system structure.

Conformed, shareable dimensions

Where the same business concept appears in multiple fact tables, we design it once as a conformed dimension. This enables drill-across reporting, ensures labels and hierarchies are consistent, and prevents the analytical silos that plague uncoordinated data marts.

Governance by design

Data quality rules, business glossary terms, lineage and stewardship are part of the model, not an afterthought. Every dimension attribute carries a definition, an owner and a quality expectation that ETL teams can implement against.

Stakeholder-led delivery

Workshops with business and technical stakeholders shape each star. We prioritise conversational modelling sessions over document-only handovers, so that the dimensional model is understood and owned by the people who will use it.

Engagement model

A typical dimensional modelling engagement moves through four phases. Each phase produces artefacts that feed the next, with stakeholder review built in throughout.

Phase 11–2 weeks
Discovery
  • • Stakeholder interviews across business domains
  • • Source system landscape review
  • • Existing warehouse / BI estate assessment
  • • Governance and regulatory constraint mapping
Phase 22–4 weeks
Design
  • • Business process selection and prioritisation
  • • Bus matrix workshop with domain leads
  • • Conceptual, logical and physical model drafts
  • • Conformed dimension and hierarchy definition
Phase 31–2 weeks
Specification
  • • Grain statements and measure definitions
  • • SCD type assignments per attribute
  • • Source-to-target mapping documentation
  • • Data quality rule and glossary alignment
Phase 4Ongoing
Handover
  • • ETL engineering walk-throughs
  • • Semantic layer and BI enablement
  • • Self-service analytics onboarding
  • • AI/ML feature store integration patterns

Public sector & regulatory alignment

The modelling approach is well-suited to public sector and regulated environments. Data models are designed to align with organisational governance frameworks and regulatory requirements — including UK GDPR, sector-specific data standards and freedom of information considerations. Conformed dimensions carry the lineage and definitions needed to support transparency reporting, while access patterns and data minimisation are reflected in the physical model design.

GDPR & Data Protection Notice

This demonstration application is designed to operate without collecting personal data from visitors. No cookies are set for analytics or tracking purposes, and no personal information is transmitted to third parties. Theme preferences are stored locally in your browser only.

In line with the UK GDPR and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2016/679), any production deployment of the underlying dimensional model must implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, including data minimisation, purpose limitation, storage limitation, access controls, and lawful basis documentation for any personal data processed within the warehouse. Data subject rights — including access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection — must be supported by the surrounding governance framework and reflected in the conformed dimension designs.

For data protection enquiries relating to this artefact, contact the repository owner via the email above. This notice is provided for demonstration purposes and should be reviewed by your Data Protection Officer before any production use.
